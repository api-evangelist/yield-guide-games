YGG’s debut title, LOL Land, launched on May 23, 2025, with over 25,000 players plunging headfirst into the chaotic fun of the browser-based casual board game in its opening weekend. Building on its many years of experience connecting Web3 player communities to the best Web3 games, the LOL Land launch also marks YGG’s expansion into game publishing. Built on Abstract Chain , the home of renowned Web3 IP Pudgy Penguins , LOL Land streamlines the reward-driven appeal of Web3 gaming into a cute, approachable package that gamers of all kinds can play anywhere, anytime.
