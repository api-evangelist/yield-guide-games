Yield Guild Games: Community Update — Q1 2025 The first few months of 2025 saw YGG unveil its Metaverse Filipino Worker campaign, publish its Perspectives in Play report, and roll out significant upgrades to the Guild Advancement Program (GAP). The early stages of the year also saw members of the YGG core team make appearances at high-profile events around the world, including ACTAI APAC , NVIDIA GTC, and GDC 2025 . GDC was a particularly active stretch for YGG, where the team co-hosted the side event Web3 @ GDC with thirdweb and unveiled YGG’s first game, LOL Land .
