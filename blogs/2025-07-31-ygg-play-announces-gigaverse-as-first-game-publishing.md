YGG Play, the publishing arm of Yield Guild Games (YGG), has brought on the onchain RPG Gigaverse as its first third-party game partner. Gigaverse and YGG Play have hit the ground running with an activation spanning both their games, including a Gigaverse-themed board in LOL Land and LOL Land-themed skins in Gigaverse. This partnership is coming off the heels of LOL Land’s launch, which laid the groundwork for YGG Play’s pioneering efforts in the Casual Degen genre.
