Following the successful launch of the game publisher, YGG Play — and the release of its first title, LOL Land — Yield Guild Games (YGG) has completed a buyback of the YGG token in line with its treasury and resource allocation policies. Using profits earned from LOL Land revenues, the 135 ETH buyback transaction was initiated on July 31, 2025, worth US$518k at the time of purchase. The 135 ETH was transferred out of the LOL Land gaming wallet into a fresh wallet for the transactions.
