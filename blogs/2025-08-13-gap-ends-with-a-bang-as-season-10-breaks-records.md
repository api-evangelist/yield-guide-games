Season 10, the final season of the Guild Advancement Program (GAP), came to a close on August 1, 2025. Over its 10-week run, the community explored the program’s signature mix of games, guild challenges, and community-building activities, ahead of the next chapter of questing with YGG. The final GAP season saw 76,841 questers, a 177.4% increase over Season 9’s quester count and the most ever in the program’s history.
