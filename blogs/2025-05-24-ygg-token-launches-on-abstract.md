Yield Guild Games (YGG) has launched the YGG token on Abstract , an Ethereum Layer 2 chain that aims to simplify Web3 onboarding through consumer-first features. Developed by Igloo Inc., the parent firm behind the Web3 IP Pudgy Penguins, Abstract has garnered significant attention from casual gamers, content creators, and crypto degens since its mainnet launch in January 2025. Abstract provides a powerful foundation for Web3 games and more with its application of chain abstraction, which makes it easier for projects to scale across ecosystems.
